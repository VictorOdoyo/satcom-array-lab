# References
- [NGA WGS84](https://earth-info.nga.mil/index.php?action=wgs84&dir=wgs84): defining ellipsoid, gravity and rotation parameters.
- [USNO approximate sidereal time](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/GAST): GMST epoch anchor and distinction between UT1 and TT.
- [CelesTrak coordinate systems](https://www.celestrak.org/columns/v02n01/) and [observer look angles](https://www.celestrak.org/columns/v02n02/): coordinate and observer geometry context.
- [MathWorks RF satellite link](https://www.mathworks.com/help/comm/ug/rf-satellite-link.html): link-budget and Doppler engineering context.
- [ITU-R P.618-14](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-P.618-14-202308-I): full Earth-space propagation prediction recommendation. The illustrative rain law here does not implement this recommendation.

Code is independently implemented. Tests and comparisons identify approximations explicitly; these references do not imply validation against measured terminal data.
