# Waveform evidence and limits
Twelve additional studies cover calibrated and uncalibrated oversampled reception, fractional timing acquisition, severe IQ mismatch, clipping, amplifier compression, phase noise, clock drift, finite-span Nyquist pulse response, image rejection, matched-filter noise and carrier-search limits.

Independent checks cover pulse energy and symmetry, removable singularities, full matched-filter delay, symbol-spaced residual ISI, interpolation against cubic polynomials, delay-filter DC gain, clock sample grids, Parseval power, IQ inversion and carrier recovery. Noiseless image rejection demonstrates algebraic cancellation and is not a hardware rejection measurement.

These waveform benches supplement the existing pass/array/link model. They do not silently convert illustrative ACM thresholds into simulated FEC performance, implement DVB-S2, add a tracking timing loop, or claim operational terminal measurements. The existing orbital and service assumptions remain in force.
